# Principles

* [P1 Business Before Technology](p-01-business-before-technology.md) - Start from the business objective; technology is an enabler, never the objective.
* [P2 Architecture Provides Direction](p-02-architecture-provides-direction.md) - Architecture gives enough direction for teams to decide independently and stay coherent.
* [P3 Governed Autonomy](p-03-governed-autonomy.md) - People, systems and AI workers act within explicit boundaries of ownership, authority and risk.
* [P4 Metadata Before Configuration](p-04-metadata-before-configuration.md) - Describe intent, ownership and behaviour as metadata; configuration executes it.
* [P5 Reuse Before Reinvention](p-05-reuse-before-reinvention.md) - Build on proven patterns and shared capabilities before introducing new approaches.
* [P6 Automation by Default](p-06-automation-by-default.md) - Automate what is repeatable, predictable and deterministic; reliable delivery is the goal.
* [P7 Security by Design](p-07-security-by-design.md) - Identity, authorization, auditing and data protection are designed in from the start.
* [P8 Observable by Design](p-08-observable-by-design.md) - Monitoring, logging, tracing, metrics and health make a system governable.
* [P9 Data as a Strategic Asset](p-09-data-as-a-strategic-asset.md) - Data is trustworthy, discoverable, governed, reusable and understandable.
* [P10 AI Requires Context](p-10-ai-requires-context.md) - AI needs authoritative information, ownership, decision boundaries and governance.
* [P11 Standards Enable Innovation](p-11-standards-enable-innovation.md) - Standards remove needless variation so effort goes to new problems.
* [P12 Continuous Improvement](p-12-continuous-improvement.md) - Learn from operational feedback; leave the platform better than you found it.
* [P13 AI-First and Colleague-Usable](p-13-ai-first-and-colleague-usable.md) - Colleagues start in plain language; the agent retrieves the smallest authoritative context and keeps humans accountable.
